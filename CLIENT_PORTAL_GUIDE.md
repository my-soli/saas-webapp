# PrintDuka Client Portal — User Guide

## Getting Started

- Go to **portal.printduka.co.ke** and log in with the email and password you were given
- If this is your first time, check your inbox for a welcome email with your login details
- Once logged in you'll land on your **Dashboard**

---

## Dashboard

- See a live summary of your orders, quotes, outstanding balance, and cart items at a glance
- All numbers update automatically — no need to refresh
- Click **New Order** to jump straight to the cart and start an order

---

## Placing an Order (Cart)

1. Click **Cart** in the left menu
2. Click **Add Products** — a product picker will open showing our full catalog
3. Tick the checkboxes next to the products you want (you can select multiple at once)
4. Set the quantity for each product using the **−** and **+** buttons in the row
5. Click **Add X items to Cart**
6. For each item in your cart you can:
   - Click the pencil icon to add a **description** (size, colour, format, etc.)
   - Add **special instructions** (e.g. "Matt laminate, double-sided")
   - Attach a **design file** from your Designs library
7. When ready, click **Place Order** in the Order Summary panel
8. Fill in the project name, delivery date, and notes (all optional)
9. Choose how you want us to reach you:
   - **Email the sales team** — we get notified automatically
   - **Share via WhatsApp** — you get a pre-filled message to send us
   - **Assign to a specific Account Manager** — pick your AM from the list
10. Click **Place Order** — your order is now live

After placing an order, you'll see a **live order link** you can copy and share. Prices are confirmed by our team in your quote within 1 business day.

---

## Tracking Your Orders

- Click **Orders** in the left menu to see all your orders
- Each order shows its current status: Submitted → Acknowledged → In Production → Ready → Shipped → Delivered
- Click any order to see the full detail page with a progress tracker, items, and invoices
- The order page refreshes automatically every 60 seconds

---

## Quotes

- Click **Quotes** to see all quotes we've prepared for you
- Quotes with status **"Sent to Customer"** are waiting for your approval
- Click a quote to review it — you can approve or request changes
- Approved quotes move into production

---

## Designs

- Click **Designs** to manage your design files (logos, artwork, photos, documents)
- Click **Upload Design** to add a new file — drag-and-drop or browse
- You can attach any uploaded design to a cart item when placing an order

---

## Documents

- Click **Documents** to see files shared by the PrintDuka team (invoices, proofs, certificates)
- You can also upload your own documents here for our team to reference
- All files can be downloaded at any time

---

## Financials

- Click **Financials** to see a summary of:
  - Total value quoted, invoiced, and paid
  - Outstanding balance and overdue invoices
  - Recent quotes, invoices, and payment history

---

## Analytics

- Click **Analytics** for a visual breakdown of your orders, quotes, and payment activity
- Use this to track your print spend and order history over time

---

## Support

- Click **Help & Tickets** to raise a support request or ask a question
- Our team typically responds within 1 business day

---

## Account & Settings

- Click **Settings** to update your profile, password, or notification preferences
- Click **Subscription** to view or manage your plan

---
